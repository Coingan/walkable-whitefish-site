Discover the Perfect Retreat

This stunning home offers the ultimate Montana getaway for up to 10 guests.

Here's what to expect:

Prime Location: Only 0.5 miles from downtown whitefish, 0.5 miles to city beach, 1 mile from Whitefish Lake Golf Club and a 15-minute drive to Whitefish Mountain Resort. Alternatively, walk 0.4 miles to the free S.N.O.W. Bus stop for easy access to mountain activities year-round.

Spacious Living: Enjoy a spacious open floor plan with a fully equipped kitchen, living area, dining area, four bedrooms, and three bathrooms. The kitchen is recently updated and features ample seating at the large island and dining table, both offering breathtaking mountain views.

Comfortable Amenities: The living area boasts a large sectional sofa and a 55" TV with Apple TV. A mudroom with washer and dryer, and two bedrooms on the main level, each with double beds and a separate full bathroom.

Master Suites: The upper level features two expansive master bedrooms. The main suite includes a king-size bed, giant walk-in closet, and an en-suite bathroom. Double French doors lead to a massive deck with cozy couches and stunning views of Whitefish Mountain. The second master has a twin bed, twin bunk beds with a trundle, an en-suite bathroom, and a large walk-in closet.

Outdoor Living: Three decks provide ample space to enjoy the mountain views, with outdoor dining tables and comfortable seating areas.

Private Yard: A fenced-in backyard in a quiet cul-de-sac ensures a peaceful and private retreat.

Convenient Access: Walkable to most amenities, with the airport just a 20-minute drive away. Two off-street parking spaces and additional street parking options are available.

This home is perfect for families, groups, or anyone seeking a luxurious and convenient base for their Whitefish adventure. Give us a call or email to book now and experience the best of Montana's outdoor paradise.
